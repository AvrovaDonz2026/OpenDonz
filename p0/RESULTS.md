## Budget and limits

The original **48 MHz / 20.833333 ns** budget is unchanged: setup/hold
uncertainty 0.5/0.1 ns, synchronous I/O maximum/minimum delay 3.0/0.5 ns,
transition 0.2 ns, output load 0.05 pF. USB line inputs `usb_d_i`, `usb_dp_i`,
`usb_dn_i` remain asynchronous/unconstrained; the checker requires their
reported paths. No false paths, multicycles or reset-check waivers are added.

The two-stage reset synchronizer has asynchronous assertion. Four actual GF180
buf_1 cells before its RN pins and the internal reset tree meet recovery/removal
**only for the existing release contract of 0.5–3 ns after a clock edge at these
two corners**. Fixed delay does not establish arbitrary-phase asynchronous
reset safety. Wait until the third rising edge after compliant release before
protocol traffic/writes. Fast corners, metastability, reset pulse width,
buffer preservation, CTS/skew and routed RC still require verification.

No RC extraction, CTS or physical signoff has been performed. Areas exclude
PHY/pads, endpoint RAM, HID/EP0 control, TL-UL/CSR, package and physical repair.
Enumeration, descriptors, CTAPHID, analog USB electrical behavior and certification
remain open. OUT streaming includes trailing CRC16 bytes; endpoint integration
must discard the tail and commit only on ACK (see README).
